OutputPRD · Prototype · Rules

Overview

服务批阅机、数智作业与智学网，面对教师、学生、运营和客服的不同诉求。我把模糊反馈转成业务规则、交互流程和验收条件，并维护从需求池到版本复盘的持续节奏。

Across marking devices, digital homework, and Zhixue, I translated needs from teachers, students, operations, and support into rules, interaction flows, and acceptance criteria—then kept the requirement-to-release loop moving.

我负责什么What I owned

  1. 需求分析Requirement framing区分表层反馈与核心任务，补齐角色、场景、规则和异常路径。Separated surface requests from core jobs, roles, rules, and exception paths.
  2. 产品表达Product specification产出 PRD、原型、业务规则与验收标准，降低协作歧义。Produced PRDs, prototypes, business rules, and acceptance criteria to reduce ambiguity.
  3. 版本运营Release operations维护需求池与版本清单，结合使用数据和反馈定位核心链路问题。Managed the pool and release list, using product data and feedback to identify friction in core journeys.

Key takeaway好需求不是写得更长，而是让不同角色对同一个问题形成同一份理解。A good requirement is not longer; it gives different roles the same understanding of the problem.

Case 03Spatial Research · 2025

Urban Villages
Left Behind?

深圳城中村公共交通空间公平性研究A spatial-equity analysis of public transit in Shenzhen

Method100m缓冲区分析buffer analysis
Metro gap500m+城中村平均更远farther on average
Bus gap≈120m城中村平均更远farther on average
Moran's I.439 / .472地铁 / 公交metro / bus
Question

深圳快速城市化过程中，城中村与正式居住区是否获得了同等水平的公共交通资源？我以单一作者身份，将社区边界、地铁、公交和住宅数据组合为可比较的空间证据。

Do Shenzhen's urban villages and formal residential areas receive equitable public-transit access? As sole author, I combined neighborhood boundaries, metro, bus, and residential data into comparable spatial evidence.

OpenStreetMapBaidu Maps APIGlobal Moran's ILISAGetis-Ord GNegative binomial regression

结果显示正式居住区的交通设施密度与分布更均衡，而城中村更容易出现网络碎片化和系统性供给不足。研究发表于 Infrastructure Reports。

The results indicate denser, more balanced provision in formal residential areas, while urban villages face more fragmented networks and systematic under-provision. Published in Infrastructure Reports.

Theme宣纸非遗 · 山水叙事Xuan paper · Landscape

Overview

项目以《千里江山图》的卷轴与山水意境为叙事线索，将宣纸非遗、现代景观与生态旅游连接起来，更新小岭村及宣纸工厂周边的公共空间。

The project uses the scroll and landscape language of A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains to connect Xuan-paper heritage, contemporary landscape, and ecological tourism around Xiaoling Village.

  2. 系统组织System design串联游客中心、文化广场、体验工坊、廊道与生态休闲区。Connected visitor, plaza, workshop, corridor, and ecological programs.
  3. 团队交付Team delivery作为组长统筹规划、建筑、景观、设施与最终视觉表达。Led planning, architecture, landscape, facilities, and final visual storytelling.
